About the club · Section 01

A student institution for entrepreneurship through acquisition.

FS SearchFund was founded in 2026 by students at Frankfurt School to give them a rigorous grounding in the search fund model and in the succession dynamics of the German, Austrian, and Swiss Mittelstand. We prepare members to find, acquire, and lead established companies – and connect them with the owners, investors, and operators who make that possible.

Focus area 01

Entrepreneurship through Acquisition

ETA describes a path in which an individual raises capital to acquire one company and then leads it as chief executive. We teach the mechanics that underpin it: search capital, proprietary sourcing, valuation and structuring, and the first hundred days after closing.

Focus area 02

Succession in the Mittelstand

A substantial share of DACH family businesses will change hands within the decade, many without an internal successor. We study how the search fund model can secure continuity of employment, culture, and regional strength – and build the relationships that make such transactions possible.

Programme

Section 03

Four recurring formats across the term. Each one moves members a step closer to being taken seriously in a room of searchers, owners, and investors.

Networking · Evening format

Fireside Chats

An acting search fund entrepreneur and an investor from the same side of the table, in a small room with a limited number of seats.

⅓
Structured questions, prepared by members
⅓
Open floor, nothing rehearsed
⅓
Drinks, and the conversations that follow
Education · Term series

Search Fund Essentials

The working craft behind a transaction, taught in the order a searcher meets it.

  • 01Finding and reading companies in the market data
  • 02How an acquisition runs, from first contact to signing
  • 03Putting a defensible number on a business
  • 04Building the deck, and holding the room while you present it
Workshop · Every two months

Searcher Pitch Competition

Members work in small searcher teams across the term. Every second month each team puts its target thesis in front of the room and defends it.

  • 01A company found, and the case for buying it
  • 02A number you are willing to defend
  • 03Questions from guests who have made the same argument to real investors
Education · Guest lecture

Deal Walkthroughs

A completed transaction taken apart step by step with the people who were in it – including the parts that nearly went wrong.

  • 01The deal retraced with the people who were in it
  • 02Written up afterwards as a short member report
  • 03Extended by our own surveys of searchers on what is currently hard
