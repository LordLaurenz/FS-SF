Definitions

03

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. Pages are delivered from Vercel’s European network and the contact form runs in Frankfurt am Main (region fra1). Because Vercel is a US company, a transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out; it is covered by Vercel’s certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and by standard contractual clauses (Art. 45 and 46 GDPR), and a data processing agreement is in place. Server logs record the requesting IP address, the date and time, and the page requested. They serve to keep the site secure and available (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) and are kept only for a short period.

04

Cookies and analytics

Only technically essential cookies are set at present. Anything beyond that – anonymised usage measurement, embedded third-party content – runs only after your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). The categories and how to withdraw consent are set out in the cookies policy.

05

Contact form and email

When you submit the contact form, the details you enter – first and last name, email address, organisation, your stated background, how you would like to contribute, and your message – are sent over an encrypted connection to a server function in Frankfurt am Main and forwarded by email to our mailbox Info@fs-searchfund.com; the website itself does not store them. We process them solely to answer you and to plan the events you offer to take part in, on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in responding (Art. 6 (1) (a) and (f) GDPR). The same applies to email you send us directly. Nothing is sold or passed to third parties for their own purposes. We delete the data once the exchange is closed and no retention obligation applies, and at your request at any time.

06

Your rights

Under Art. 15–21 GDPR you may request access to your data, its correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Consent once given may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – for us that is the Hessischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Wiesbaden.

07

Minors

The service is aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under sixteen. If you believe a minor has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.
