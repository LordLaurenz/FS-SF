Legal · Accessibility

Accessibility statement.

A voluntary commitment. As an unincorporated student initiative we are not a public body and are not subject to the BITV or the Hessian accessibility regulation – we hold ourselves to this standard because we think a site nobody can use is a site not worth building.

Imprint Privacy Cookies Accessibility
01

Scope

This statement applies to the website of FS SearchFund, an independent student initiative in Frankfurt am Main.

02

Standard we work to

We aim at WCAG 2.1 level AA as a self-imposed target. The site is currently partly conformant: most of it meets the standard, some parts do not yet. No external audit has been carried out.

03

Known limitations

  • DocumentsPublished papers are not yet tagged for screen readers
  • Embedded mediaThird-party content we embed is outside our control
  • Plain languageNo easy-read or sign-language version is available
04

Reporting a barrier

Tell us what did not work and we will fix it, or find another way to get you the content. Write to Laurenz Lange at hello@laurenzlange.de. We aim to reply within four weeks. If a document is inaccessible to you, we will send it in a readable form.

05

How this was assessed

Drawn up on 20 September 2026 on the basis of a self-assessment. It is reviewed once a year and after any substantial redesign.
